# Part 2 — Why Proxy Statements (DEF 14A) Are the "Did" Document

The task allows several formal-disclosure sources. I use the annual
proxy statement (SEC Form DEF 14A; DEFC14A in contested years), for
four reasons:

1. **Universal coverage.** Filing a proxy statement is mandatory
   for every public company holding an annual meeting, so all 50
   sample firms have one in all nine years (450 of 450 collected).
   The main alternative, standalone ESG / sustainability / DEI
   reports, is voluntary: which firms publish them, and since when,
   is itself a choice correlated with the values being measured,
   which would build selection into the sample.
2. **A stable, dated structure.** Proxies are filed on a known
   calendar (mostly spring, for the fiscal year just ended) with a
   stable section structure, so year-over-year comparison measures
   language change rather than format change.
3. **The board's formal account.** The proxy is where the board
   accounts to shareholders for governance, executive pay, human
   capital and its response to shareholder proposals. It is the
   closest formal counterpart to the values an About page
   advertises, and it is produced under legal review, which makes
   the "said vs disclosed" contrast in Part 3 meaningful.
4. **Machine-readable at scale.** EDGAR provides full text through
   a stable API for the whole sample and period; the pipeline
   scales to 500 firms without new sources.

Alternatives considered and set aside: 10-K human-capital
disclosure (required only from 2020, brief, and buried in the
business description); CSR reports (no standard form, calendar or
coverage); earnings-call transcripts (spoken, unscripted in part,
and not a board account).

Known limitation of the choice: the proxy is a governance genre,
so compensation and compliance vocabulary dominates its volume,
and theme emphasis there means something narrower than on an About
page. Part 3 handles this by standardising within document type
and interpreting alignment relatively, never by comparing raw
levels across the two genres.
